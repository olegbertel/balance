Balance Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

1. General Information

Balance is a personal finance tracking app that allows users to manage accounts, income, expenses, categories, and payment methods.

We respect users' privacy. In the current version of Balance, financial data is stored locally on the user's device. The app does not use its own server, user registration, advertising networks, or third-party analytics services.

2. Data Processed by the App

Balance allows users to create and store financial data, including accounts and their balances, income and expenses, categories, subcategories, payment methods, and other information they enter into the app to manage their personal finances.

This data is stored locally on the user's device. In the current version of Balance, the developer does not automatically access this data through its own server.

3. Sharing Data with Third Parties

Balance does not transmit the financial information users enter into the app to the developer or to third-party advertising or analytics services. The app does not use the developer's servers to store or process this data.

Purchases and Balance Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple may process information necessary to handle payments, manage subscriptions, and prevent fraud in accordance with its terms and privacy policy.

Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Financial data is stored locally on the user's device. Users can manage and delete individual records using the features available in the app.

Deleting the app removes locally stored app data from the device. However, copies saved in device backups may remain.

Apple may retain purchase and subscription information in accordance with its policies and applicable legal requirements.

5. Synchronization and Cloud Services

The current version of Balance does not support synchronization of financial data through iCloud or other cloud services.

If new methods of data storage, synchronization, or processing are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes are implemented.

6. Children's Privacy

Balance is not specifically designed for children and does not ask users to provide personal information to create an account.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app, its features, or applicable requirements. The current version will be available at a public URL.
